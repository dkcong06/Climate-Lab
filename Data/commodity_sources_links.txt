Links grouped by output CSV. Primary source for these series is FRED (Federal Reserve Economic Data);
underlying spot definitions are documented on each FRED series page (often sourced from EIA).

Actual observations are stored in the four tidy CSVs below (columns: observation_date, fred_series_id,
value, units). Regenerate them with:
  python scripts/export_catalog_timeseries.py

Series definitions / spans / regions are in: data/catalog_series_definitions.csv
